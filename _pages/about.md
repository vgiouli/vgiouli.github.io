I am an Assistant Professor of Computational Linguistics at the Department of German Language and Literature, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Greece.

My research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP), computational lexical semantics, computational lexicography, and the development of multilingual language resources and technologies.

## Research Interests

- **Frame Semantics and Semantic Role Labelling**
- **Multiword Expressions (MWEs)**
- **Language Technologies and Large Language Models (LLMs)**
- **Computational Approaches to Emotion and Subjectivity**

## Research Projects

My current research activities include the development of **FrameNet-el**, a FrameNet resource for Modern Greek, and international collaborations focusing on multiword expressions, language technologies, and AI-supported language learning.

## Teaching

At Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, I teach courses in Computational Linguistics, Computational Lexicography, Electronic Lexicography, and Language Technologies and Digital Humanities.
