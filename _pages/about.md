# About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Computational Linguistics at the Department of German Language and Literature, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Greece.

My research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP), computational lexical semantics, computational lexicography, and the development of multilingual language resources and technologies.

My current research interests include:

- **Frame Semantics and Semantic Role Labelling**, with particular emphasis on the development of FrameNet resources for Modern Greek.
- **Multiword Expressions (MWEs)**, their linguistic representation, computational processing, and integration into multilingual lexical resources.
- **Language Technologies and Large Language Models (LLMs)**, including applications in education, digital humanities, and multilingual NLP.
- **Computational Approaches to Emotion and Subjectivity**, including emotion recognition, toxicity detection, and trustworthy AI.

My work combines linguistic theory, computational methods, and language resource development, with an emphasis on multilingualism and under-resourced languages.

## Research Projects

My current research activities include the development of **FrameNet-el**, a FrameNet resource for Modern Greek, and participation in international research collaborations focusing on multiword expressions, language technologies, and AI-supported language learning.

## Teaching

At Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, I teach courses in Computational Linguistics, Computational Lexicography, Electronic Lexicography, and Language Technologies and Digital Humanities.
